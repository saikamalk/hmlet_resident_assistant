# House Rules & Policies

## Booking & Contracts

* Minimum stay depends on the property and room type. It can be anywhere from 1 to 6 months, so check the listing for
  the exact minimum stay.
* A security deposit equal to one month's rent is required at the time of booking. The deposit is refunded within 14
  business days after move-out, following the inspection and after any applicable deductions.
* If you want to extend your contract, please let us know at least 30 days before the current contract ends. This will
  depend on room availability.
* Early move-in can be arranged up to 5 days before the lease start date if the room is ready and the request has been
  approved. There is no additional charge for this.

## Cancellations

* If you cancel more than 14 days before your move-in date, the full deposit will be refunded.
* If you cancel within 14 days of the move-in date, 50% of the deposit will be forfeited.
* After move-in, the normal 30-day notice period applies.

## Payments & Billing

* Rent is due on the 1st of every month.
* There is a 3-day grace period. After that, a $50 late fee (or equivalent in local currency) will apply.
* Electricity, water and Wi-Fi are included in the rent unless the listing specifically mentions otherwise.
* Receipts for tax or reimbursement purposes can be requested at any time. These are normally issued within 3 business
  days.

## Notice & Move-Out

* Residents need to give 30 days' written notice before moving out. This can be done through the resident portal or by
  informing the property manager.
* Room changes within the same property are possible if another room is available. A new agreement will need to be
  signed and the rate of the new room will apply from the date of the switch.
* There is no separate room-switching fee.

## Maintenance

For any maintenance issue, please raise a request through the resident portal or support chat. Please include the
property name and room number.

* Non-urgent issues are normally handled within 3–5 business days.
* Urgent issues are given priority and should be addressed within 24 hours. This includes things like no water, no
  electricity, broken locks or other security issues, and health hazards such as mold.

## Pets

Pets are allowed only at properties that are pet-friendly.
If you are not sure whether a particular property allows pets, please check with the property manager before making any
arrangements.

## Noise

Quiet hours are from 10 PM to 8 AM.
If there is a noise issue, report it through the resident portal and the property team will follow up with the resident
involved.

---

## Guidelines for Resident Support (Internal)

* Don't share another resident's personal information, room details, billing information or ticket history.
* Don't confirm that a cancellation, refund, contract extension or any other contract change has been completed unless
  the resident has confirmed the action.
* For financial questions, only explain the policies mentioned above. Don't provide additional financial advice.
* If a request is not related to resident support, redirect it to the appropriate channel.
